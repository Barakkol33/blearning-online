# High school: the bagrut process and the plan

The rules the high-school courses are planned by, from the Ministry of Education's documents for 2026–27 (תשפ"ז). Checked 2026-11-27.

## The process

- **The new program.** Starting תשפ"ז, every student sits only the papers of the new program. It was piloted from summer 2024.
- **Three levels:** 3, 4 and 5 units (יח״ל).
  - Students are placed at the end of grade 9.
  - **3 units:** students who learned the reduced grade-9 track.
  - **4 units:** students from the middle levels. The Ministry wants more students at 4 than today.
  - **5 units:** strong students with motivation.
  - The Ministry asks schools to place as many students as possible at 4–5.
  - There is a safety net when moving down a level, from 5 to 4 or from 4 to 3.
- **Papers.** Grade 10 teaches material that is examined later. Each level has one paper in grade 11 and one in grade 12. Each grade-11 paper covers the material of grades 10 and 11.

  | Level | Grade 10 | Grade 11 | Grade 12 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 3 units | school assessment, paper 35173 (alternative assessment, tasks) | 35371 | 35372 |
  | 4 units | no paper | 35471 | 35472 |
  | 5 units | no paper | 35571 (first part: short questions) | 35572 |

- **Order.** The order in the teaching recommendations is a suggestion. Teachers may change it.
- **Shared start.** The first topics of 4 and 5 units are aligned, to make moving between levels easier.
- **Full written solutions.** Bagrut solutions must show every step, including the substitution into formulas.
  - A bare final answer, or skipped steps, can be disqualified.
  - "I used a calculator" is not accepted as an appeal.
  - So our model solutions always show every step.
- **The 5-unit plus.** 5-unit students who pass 35571 can take an academic first-semester calculus course in parallel.

## Placement in grade 10

- **3 units:** grade 10 is assessed at school. The material is organized in clusters, not chapters:
  - science and society;
  - space and shape;
  - finance and economics.

  Algebra technique is taught only when a context needs it.
- **4 and 5 units:** each runs two topics in parallel, for example analytic geometry next to precalculus (properties of functions before calculus).
  - **Grade 10 completes grade-9 gaps.** At 4 units this means ratios of sides and angles in a triangle, midsegments, area ratios of similar triangles, and second-degree systems of equations.
  - **Parameters.** At 4 units, use few parameters in grade 10 calculus. They grow in grade 11.
